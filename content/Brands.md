**Allmer Brands** is the division of [[Simon Allmer Entertainment]] responsible for the management, presentation, and long-term development of all story worlds created by the company. It serves as a central registry of intellectual properties — from single-medium works to expansive, cross-media universes — ensuring that every brand is represented consistently, meaningfully, and with artistic integrity.

In addition to **Brand Management**, Allmer Brands leads outreach and engagement efforts for key franchises. This includes hyperlocal marketing initiatives, partnerships with creators and influencers, and appearances at conventions or expos. It also develops and supervises Extensions— non-studio projects such as podcasts, community initiatives, and interactive experiences that deepen audience connection without belonging to a single production studio.
